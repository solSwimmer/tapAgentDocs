Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 14, 2026

This policy describes how solSwimmer (“we”) handles information in the Android app Tap Agent (package com.solswimmer.tapagent).

Tap Agent lets you create touch maps and dispatch programmatic tap events to other apps, using an optional on-screen control panel.

Short version

Tap Agent does not create an account. Touch maps and settings stay on your device and are not uploaded to solSwimmer. The accessibility service is used only to send tap gestures — it does not read screen content or take screenshots.

The free app shows ads through Google AdMob (Google’s advertising platform). Google may collect device and advertising identifiers to serve and measure those ads. solSwimmer does not receive your touch maps or a personal profile from that process.

Information we collect

solSwimmer does not operate an account system and does not receive your touch maps or a name, email, or payment card through the app itself.

We do not use a separate analytics or crash-reporting SDK. We do use the Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) SDK to show ads.

Information stored on your device

The app stores the following locally in app private storage:

  • Touch maps you create (names, coordinates, timing, and related settings), as JSON files
  • Which map is selected and whether the service was left enabled

This data is used only to run the features you configure. It is not uploaded to solSwimmer.

If Android Backup is enabled on your device, Android may include this local app data in your device backup (for example to Google Drive). That backup is controlled by your Google / device settings, not by a solSwimmer server.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Tap Agent uses Google AdMob to display ads. When ads are requested or shown, Google and its advertising partners may process information such as:

  • Advertising ID (Google Advertising ID) and other device identifiers
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • Device and app data (model, OS version, app version)
  • Ad views, clicks, and whether an ad request succeeded

That processing is done by Google under Google’s Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services. How partners use data is also described in AdMob’s advertising partner guidance.

In the European Economic Area, UK, and similar regions we use Google’s User Messaging Platform to ask for consent (or legitimate-interest choices) before personalized ads. You can also reset or opt out of personalized ads in Android Settings → Google → Ads (wording varies by device).

If we later offer a paid option to remove ads, that purchase would be processed by Google Play, not by solSwimmer directly. We would still need the network only if ads remain in the free version you are using.

Permissions and sensitive capabilities

Tap Agent requests only what it needs to work:

  • Accessibility service. Used solely to dispatch tap gestures you start (dispatchGesture). Window content is not retrieved. Screenshots are not taken. The service does not read text, view hierarchy, or other apps’ content. You can turn it off any time in Android Settings → Accessibility.
  • Display over other apps. Used to show the floating control panel, touch-area markers, and optional overlay while the service is running. The app does not use this permission to capture what is on the screen.
  • Notifications. Used for the foreground-service status notification while Tap Agent is running.
  • Run at startup. If you left the service enabled, the app may restart in standby after a reboot. Automation does not resume until you start a map.
  • Foreground service / keep awake. Used so the control panel and event dispatch can continue while you use other apps.
  • Internet and network state. Used to request and display ads from Google. Not used to upload your touch maps.

Information we do not access

  • Contacts, photos, files outside the app, location, microphone, or camera
  • Screen contents, screenshots, or other apps’ text or UI trees
  • Your Google account or payment information inside the app (none are collected by us today)

Sharing

solSwimmer does not sell your information and does not share your touch maps with anyone. Maps never leave the device through our code.

Advertising data is processed by Google (AdMob) and, depending on your consent choices, Google’s advertising partners. If you install Tap Agent from Google Play, Google may also process install and device information under Google’s Privacy Policy.

Children

Tap Agent is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

  • Delete individual touch maps in the app
  • Turn off the accessibility service or overlay permission in system Settings
  • Uninstall the app to delete local maps and settings from the device
  • Limit or reset advertising ID / opt out of personalized ads in Android Google Ads settings
  • In supported regions, change ad consent through the in-app consent form (or reinstall to see it again)

Because we do not hold a copy of your data, we cannot remotely export or erase device-local maps. Uninstall or in-app delete is the way to remove them.

Changes

If we add features that change this policy — for example cloud sync, map sharing, or a paid “remove ads” purchase — we will update this page and the effective date. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy.
