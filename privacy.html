Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 14, 2026

This policy describes how Sol Swimming (“we”) handles information in the Android app Tap Agent (package com.solswimmer.tapagent). Sol Swimming is the developer name on Google Play. We also use the brand name solSwimmer.

Tap Agent lets you create touch maps and dispatch programmatic tap events to other apps, using an optional on-screen control panel.

Short version

Tap Agent does not create an account. Touch maps and settings are stored in the app’s private storage on your device. Sol Swimming does not operate a server that receives those maps.

The accessibility service is used only to dispatch tap gestures you start. It is not configured to read screen content, take screenshots, or observe other apps’ user input.

The ad-supported app uses Google AdMob to show ads. Google — not Sol Swimming — operates that advertising service. What Google and its partners process can vary by region, consent choices, and AdMob configuration. See Google’s Privacy Policy.

Who this app is for

Tap Agent is intended for adult users of Android automation tools. It is not designed for children, is not a children’s app, and we do not treat children under 13 as an audience. This should match the target-audience setting in Google Play Console (ages 13+ or 18+, not younger).

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact solSwimmer1820@proton.me and we will delete anything we actually hold (today, that would not include touch maps, because we do not receive them).

What Sol Swimming receives

Sol Swimming does not operate user accounts and does not receive your touch maps, name, email, or payment card through the app.

We do not embed a separate analytics or crash-reporting library (for example Firebase Analytics or Crashlytics). If you install Tap Agent from Google Play, Google may still receive crashes and performance data through Android vitals as part of Play, under Google’s Privacy Policy. Sol Swimming may see aggregated or de-identified vitals in Play Console; that is Google’s collection, not an SDK we added.

Information stored on your device

The app stores the following locally in app-private storage:

  • Touch maps you create (names, coordinates, timing, and related settings), as JSON files
  • Which map is selected and whether the service was left enabled

This data is used only to run the features you configure. The Tap Agent application does not transmit touch-map contents to Sol Swimming or to advertising providers.

Android Backup

The app allows Android Auto Backup (android:allowBackup="true"). If Backup is enabled in your device / Google account settings, Android may include this local app data in your cloud or device-to-device backup (for example Google Drive).

That is a backup of your device, operated by Google or the device maker. Sol Swimming does not control those backups, does not receive a copy of them, and cannot read or delete them. Turn off Android Backup or uninstall the app if you do not want maps included in a backup you control.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The ad-supported version of Tap Agent uses the Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) SDK to request and display ads. Sol Swimming does not receive a personal advertising profile from Google as part of showing those ads. Touch maps are not sent to AdMob by the Tap Agent application.

Independently of Sol Swimming, Google and participating advertising partners may process advertising-related data when an ad is requested or shown. What is processed is not an exhaustive list and can depend on SDK version, ad format, your region, consent or other privacy choices, and Google’s then-current configuration. It may include, for example:

  • Advertising ID and other device or app identifiers
  • IP address and approximate location derived from network information
  • Device and app metadata (such as model, OS version, app version)
  • Ad serving and interaction signals (such as requests, impressions, and clicks)

Google describes that processing in Google’s Privacy Policy, How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services, and AdMob advertising partner information.

Where required by applicable law or by Google’s advertising requirements, the app presents Google’s User Messaging Platform so you can make ad choices. Whether ads are personalized, limited, or not shown can depend on those choices and on Google’s systems. You can also reset or opt out of personalized ads in Android Settings → Google → Ads (labels vary by device).

If we later offer a paid option to remove ads, that purchase would be processed by Google Play, not by Sol Swimming collecting card details.

Permissions and capabilities

These match the current Android manifest and accessibility configuration. Human names first; system names in parentheses.

  • Accessibility service (BIND_ACCESSIBILITY_SERVICE; canPerformGestures="true"; canRetrieveWindowContent="false"; canTakeScreenshot="false"). Used solely to dispatch tap gestures you start (dispatchGesture). The service is not configured to retrieve window content or take screenshots. It does not record or transmit other apps’ user gestures, text, or UI trees. Coordinates you save in a touch map are stored only in local app files. You can turn the service off in Android Settings → Accessibility.
  • Display over other apps (SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW). Used to show the floating control panel, touch-area markers, and optional overlay. Not used to capture what is on the screen.
  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS). Used for the foreground-service status notification.
  • Restart after reboot (RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED). This is not an overlay or accessibility permission. If you left the service enabled, a boot receiver may start Tap Agent in standby after reboot. Automation does not resume until you start a map.
  • Foreground service (FOREGROUND_SERVICE, FOREGROUND_SERVICE_SPECIAL_USE) and keep awake (WAKE_LOCK). Used so the control panel and event dispatch can continue while you use other apps.
  • Internet (INTERNET, and if present ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE). Used to request and display ads from Google when the ad-supported build includes AdMob. Not used by Tap Agent to upload touch maps.

Information we do not access

  • Contacts, photos, files outside the app, precise location, microphone, or camera
  • Screen contents, screenshots, or other apps’ text or UI trees
  • Other apps’ live touch or gesture input
  • Your Google account password or payment card numbers inside Tap Agent

Sharing

Sol Swimming does not sell your information and does not share your touch maps with third parties. The Tap Agent application does not transmit touch-map contents to Sol Swimming or to advertising providers.

Advertising-related data may be processed by Google (AdMob) and, depending on your privacy choices and Google’s configuration, by Google’s advertising partners. If you install from Google Play, Google may also process install, device, and vitals information under Google’s Privacy Policy.

For Google Play’s Data safety form, “collection” and “sharing” include what SDKs in the app do — not only what Sol Swimming’s servers receive. AdMob declarations should follow Google’s then-current AdMob / Data safety guidance.

Your choices

  • Delete individual touch maps in the app
  • Turn off the accessibility service or overlay permission in system Settings
  • Uninstall the app to delete local maps and settings from the device
  • Limit or reset your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in Android Google Ads settings
  • Where a consent form is shown, use it to change ad choices

Because Sol Swimming does not hold a copy of your maps, we cannot remotely export or erase device-local maps. Uninstall or in-app delete is the way to remove them.

Changes

If we add features that change this policy — for example cloud sync, map sharing, or a paid “remove ads” purchase — we will update this page and the effective date. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy.
